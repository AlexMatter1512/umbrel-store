Pair Surf, open your dashboard, and leave the browser work to Umbrel.

Connecting to Surf…

1. Choose your dashboard

This URL opens on Umbrel. For Home Assistant on the same Umbrel, use 127.0.0.1:8123. Paste your dashboard path, such as /dashboard-tablet/0.

Recommended: Umbrel's LAN IP followed by :18080. Leave blank to use the hostname you opened this page with.

Restarts Surf and changes the active tab. The iPad reconnects; logins and paired devices are kept.

2. Pair your iPad

Open Surf on your iPad and add a server. Create a code here, then enter the address and code on the iPad.

Compare the six words shown here and on your iPad. Confirm on the iPad only when they match.

3. Sign in to Home Assistant

Once paired, the dashboard opens in Surf on the iPad. Sign in there with your Home Assistant account, then bookmark your dashboard and enable fullscreen in Surf.

Home Assistant login cookies are saved on Umbrel in Surf's browser profile. Keep the iPad and Umbrel on the same reachable network.
